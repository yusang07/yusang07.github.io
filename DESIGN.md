# Personal Website Design Direction

## Theme: Circuitry meets imagination

This portfolio uses a quiet editorial design inspired by a research notebook and a semiconductor circuit. It should feel thoughtful, modern, and personal rather than like a generic technology dashboard.

- **Mood:** warm, precise, curious, and slightly experimental
- **Palette:** paper cream `#f5f0e7`, deep forest `#13261f`, moss `#5e7462`, electric lime `#c9f04b`
- **Typography:** Playfair Display for expressive Korean headings, Gowun Dodum for Korean body text, DM Mono for labels and metadata
- **Layout:** generous whitespace, thin divider lines, numbered sections, large editorial typography
- **Motifs:** orbital circuit shapes, layered book pages, small geometric sparks; use CSS shapes rather than heavy images
- **Interaction:** gentle orbit movement and smooth anchor scrolling; keep all content readable and motion subtle
- **Responsive behavior:** single-column sections on small screens, readable large type, and touch-friendly navigation

Avoid gradients, stock photos, dense card grids, and generic blue "tech" styling. Keep the design accessible by maintaining strong text contrast and preserving clear content hierarchy.
